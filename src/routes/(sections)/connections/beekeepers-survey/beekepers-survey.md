The Beekeepers' Survey is an exploration of the intricate relationships between humans and bees, inspired by Charles Butler's A Feminine Monarchie. This project delves into the interdisciplinary connections between science, literature, music, and history, examining how bees have been perceived, studied, and represented across time. By revisiting Butler's work with a diverse team of experts, the survey seeks to uncover new insights into the emotional, cultural, and ecological significance of bees, offering a unique perspective on the interplay between human creativity and the natural world.
