Molecular biologists have been studying whether honeybees could be useful in forensic science. Proteins in honey show which flowers bees have visited and the compounds in those flowers. Just as we can detect traces of plant pesticides in honey, so a team at George Mason University have been studying whether traces of human remains (volatile organic compounds) in honey could help with police investigations.
