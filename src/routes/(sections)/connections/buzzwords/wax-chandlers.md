The Worshipful Company of Wax Chandlers has a lot of interesting material on the history of wax laws, and they themselves have a very early modern history. [This page](https://www.waxchandlers.org.uk/about/wax-chandler-ordinances-of-1371/) explains all about the way early wax history interlinked with church history. The wax ordinance of 1371 guards against 'deceitful mixtures' and lists fines for the selling of adulterated wax.
